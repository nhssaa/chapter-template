# Common data sources for chapters

All free and legal to use for learning. Link to these; do not copy the data into git.

## Multi-sport starters (NHSSAA's own)
- [nhssaa/sports-data-starters](https://github.com/nhssaa/sports-data-starters) - clone-and-go datasets and starter models for soccer, football, baseball, hockey and cricket.
- [nhssaa/xg-for-beginners](https://github.com/nhssaa/xg-for-beginners) - a first expected-goals model from 8,000+ real shots, browser-based.

## Soccer
- FBref (https://fbref.com) - team and player stats, scraping allowed at polite rates.
- Understat (https://understat.com) - shot-level xG data.
- StatsBomb Open Data (https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data) - free event data.

## American football
- nflverse (https://github.com/nflverse) - play-by-play, rosters, Next Gen Stats.

## Baseball
- Lahman database (https://www.seanlahman.com) - full MLB history as CSVs.
- Baseball Savant (https://baseballsavant.mlb.com) - Statcast search and CSV export.

## Hockey
- Hockey Reference (https://www.hockey-reference.com) - season and game stats.
- MoneyPuck (http://moneypuck.com) - shot-level and xG data as CSVs.

## Cricket
- Cricsheet (https://cricsheet.org) - ball-by-ball data for thousands of matches.

## Basketball
- Basketball Reference (https://www.basketball-reference.com) - NBA stats.
